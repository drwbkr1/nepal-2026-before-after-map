## New processing-baseline finding

The earlier review named `N0512` versus `N0513` as a general risk. ESA's [PB 05.13 announcement](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/news/2026-8-7-introduction-copernicus-sentinel-2-processing-baseline-0513) identifies changes to L2A no-data boundaries, cast-shadow classification, surface reflectance at swath edges and over water, saturation metadata, JPEG2000 encoding, and spectral-response metadata. ESA [scheduled deployment for 21 September](https://sentinels.copernicus.eu/-/deployment-of-copernicus-sentinel-2-processing-baseline-05.13-on-21-september-2026), and says new products use PSD v15.2. The project's existing [GDAL optical reader](../scripts/m2_optical_gdal_recovery_002_engine.py) explicitly requires **05.12 for both products**, so it cannot simply be rerun on this exact candidate. The [method-risk record](../records/observations/m2-optical-pb0513-method-risk-local-001.json) preserves the official findings and code constraint.

The revised [proposal](../contracts/milestone-002-optical-alternate-map-pair-002-proposal.json) therefore requires a **separate PB 05.13-aware candidate reader** and generated-data tests before any protected read. It does not modify or relax the old 05.12 reader or terminal results. Each product must agree internally with its exact name baseline; BOA offsets and quantification must come from its own metadata. The same class-number exclusion list is retained, but different no-data and SCL algorithms must be reported as a method uncertainty, never as landscape change. No radiometric harmonization, reflectance difference, index-change map, or quantitative baseline is authorized.
