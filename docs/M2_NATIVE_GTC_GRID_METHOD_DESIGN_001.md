# M2 native-GTC grid method design 001

**Status:** Local design candidate; no implementation, ArcPy call, protected-data read, publication, or new attempt authorized. This design refines the [map-oriented route review](M2_MAP_ORIENTED_ROUTE_REVIEW_001.md), not the closed attempts or frozen QA contract.

## Question and evidence

Can the exact ascending M1-SRC-002/005 SAR sequence produce native GTC rasters with credible georeferencing and retained source detail, then yield the frozen EPSG:32645, 10 m analysis grid? The saved M1-SRC-002 GTC gamma, dB, and mask rasters instead report EPSG:4326, 2 × 2 cells, 10 degrees per cell. The input slant and despeckled CRFs report 25,769 × 16,732 cells over approximately 83.798–86.856°E and 27.521–29.598°N. The approved 11-tile DEM mosaic reports 14,400 × 10,800 EPSG:4326 cells. These metadata do not show usable radar pixels or prove a cause. [Terminal metadata](../records/readiness/m2-radar-event-area-pair-grid-provenance-recovery-001-real-001-terminal-reconciliation.json) · [DEM mosaic](../records/processing/m2-radar-event-area-pair-001-dem-mosaic-reconciliation.json)

The runner currently creates a 2 × 2 **projected** 10 m snap raster, then wraps each native GTC call in an environment containing that snap and numeric `cellSize=10.0`. It sets neither an output CRS nor an extent in those two GTC scopes. The numeric value 10.0 equals the observed geographic cell size, but the tool's documented environment list includes Snap Raster and not Cell Size. This supports a unit-interaction hypothesis, not a historical diagnosis. [Code-and-arithmetic record](../records/observations/m2-gtc-grid-unit-hypothesis-001.json) · [Esri GTC documentation](https://doc.esri.com/en/arcgis-pro/latest/tool-reference/image-analyst/apply-geometric-terrain-correction.html)

## One candidate change, pending review

In a **new** append-only runner, retain the exact source IDs, orbit files, eleven-tile ellipsoidal DEM, `GEOID NONE`, VV/VH, thermal-noise removal, beta calibration, gamma terrain flattening, and approved `REFINED_LEE` sequence. Before each native GTC call, capture the active ArcPy settings and clear `cellSize`, `snapRaster`, `outputCoordinateSystem`, and `extent` to defaults. Record the cleared values before the call. Keep the approved continuous-versus-categorical resampling distinction. Use `try/finally` or a dedicated worker lifecycle so a failure cannot leak modified settings into later stages; verify restoration in a disposable ArcGIS test. Do not silently change the DEM, use tie-point-only terrain correction, or add a geographic degree-size guess.

Esri documents `ClearEnvironment(name)` as resetting one setting to its default and `EnvManager` as restoring settings it changes when its scope exits. Whether this precise isolation changes GTC output, or whether it changes any hidden ArcGIS behavior, is **unverified**. The implementation would need a runtime test of the clearing and restoration semantics before source processing. [ClearEnvironment](https://doc.esri.com/en/arcgis-pro/latest/arcpy/functions/clearenvironment.html) · [EnvManager](https://doc.esri.com/en/arcgis-pro/latest/arcpy/classes/envmanager.html) · [environment inheritance](https://doc.esri.com/en/arcgis-pro/latest/arcpy/geoprocessing_and_python/using-environment-settings.html)

The 11-tile DEM mosaic has 155,520,000 cells, but its structure does not establish valid elevation over either actual processed SAR extent. The [recovery-003 proposal](../contracts/milestone-002-radar-event-area-pair-native-gtc-grid-recovery-003-proposal.json) now proposes a native cap of 200 million cells, 2 GiB logical bytes, and 10 GiB on disk per output, plus a gross cell-size stop at one arc second plus 0.000001 degree. The cap is about 1.286 times the DEM mosaic's cell count, and a two-band 32-bit float raster at that cap has 1.6 billion raw sample bytes. Those are **proposed** resource and method predicates, not observed GTC fitness. The previously used 100,000,000-cell projected cap and 100 m extension guard remain resource stops, not scientific acceptance thresholds. [DEM reconciliation](../records/processing/m2-radar-event-area-pair-001-dem-mosaic-reconciliation.json) · [closed projection result](../records/readiness/m2-radar-event-area-pair-projection-extent-recovery-001-disposable-validation-terminal.json)

ArcPy's Raster documentation exposes `width`, `height`, `meanCellWidth`, `meanCellHeight`, `extent`, `spatialReference`, `bandCount`, and `isTemporary` on Raster objects; its list of temporary-raster properties that may be `None` does not include those grid fields. A candidate guard can therefore inspect the Raster **returned by GTC before calling `save`**, persist the declared grid, and reject gross size or unit errors before materializing a permanent CRF. This API capability is documented generally, but availability and cost for this particular GTC return must be proven on disposable inputs and observed in the approved SAR attempt; it is not evidence that the old GTC output was valid. [Esri Raster object](https://doc.esri.com/en/arcgis-pro/latest/arcpy/classes/raster-object.html)

## Remaining execution uncertainty

The proposal now fixes a gross native-grid screen and resource ceilings for owner review. A passing screen would not prove 10 m effective source detail; the native resolution must be disclosed after observation, and the frozen registration and pixel QA still apply. The documented Raster-object properties offer a possible pre-save guard, but its GTC-specific behavior is untested. Full-extent DEM valid pixels and an independent supervisor that retains terminal evidence if ArcGIS stalls or crashes remain conditional gates inside the proposed envelope. A generic raster cannot simulate SAR range-Doppler backgeocoding, so disposable success alone cannot justify a claim that the new GTC method works. Approval of this design document alone is not requested; the exact recovery-003 proposal and bundle are the decision objects.

The consumed `e1/a1` attempt, its partial projection and zero-byte receipts remain immutable. The two failed disposable projection/clip candidates remain closed. Nothing here permits reused outputs, acquisition, another source or date, changed AOIs or thresholds, baseline admission, change analysis, interpretation, attribution, derived-pixel publication, or scientific publication.
