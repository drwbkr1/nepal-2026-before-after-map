**Status:** Local, zero-decision source and method research, 26 September 2026. ## Why this route matters

The ascending M1-SRC-002/005 Sentinel-1D GRD pair has the strongest catalog footprint match to both event AOIs. The local ArcGIS GTC route has not yielded usable pixels: the retained old output is a 2 × 2 geographic grid, native-GTC recovery-003 stopped at a disposable projected-extent guard, a later real `e1/a3` attempt stopped at `native_declared_resource_limit_exceeded` on its first source, and the TIFF/Warp recovery-004 then stopped at a reused attempt identity before another real process. The TIFF bridge cannot solve an upstream native-GTC resource stop. [Map-route terminal](M2_MAP_ROUTE_FEASIBILITY_001_TERMINAL.md) · [Recovery-004 terminal](M2_RADAR_EVENT_AREA_PAIR_GTC_TIFF_WARP_RECOVERY_004_TERMINAL.md)

ASF documents an **on-demand full-scene radiometric terrain-corrected (RTC)** Sentinel-1 product generated with GAMMA software. It is delivered as UTM GeoTIFFs with VV/VH and a layover/shadow mask; options include 10 m pixels and gamma0 in dB. That is a plausible way to obtain ArcGIS-readable spatial pixels without executing local GTC. It is a **new processed product and scientific method**, not an equivalent replacement for the approved ArcGIS sequence. ASF's documentation names Sentinel-1D in its output convention and supports IW GRD-H inputs, but the actual HyP3 acceptance of these two scenes remains untested. [ASF product guide](https://hyp3-docs.asf.alaska.edu/guides/rtc_product_guide/) · [ASF Vertex eligibility](https://hyp3-docs.asf.alaska.edu/using/vertex/)

## Competing CDSE on-demand route, assessed without an account request

The CDSE `SENTINEL-1-RTC` archive query above is distinct from the [Sentinel Hub Process API](https://documentation.dataspace.copernicus.eu/APIs/SentinelHub/Process.html). The latter documents on-demand Sentinel-1 GRD processing with `GAMMA0_TERRAIN` and orthorectification, plus VV/VH, local-incidence, scattering-area, shadow, and data masks. It uses Copernicus DEM and source-embedded orbit state vectors. That is another **new method**, not a continuation of the approved local ArcGIS processing. [CDSE S1GRD method](https://documentation.dataspace.copernicus.eu/APIs/SentinelHub/Data/S1GRD.html)

For the project's frozen 9,652-column by 8,098-row EPSG:32645 radar frame, the documented 2,500-pixel limit in each Process API output dimension implies at least 4 by 4 tiles per date, or 32 successful image requests for two dates, before any seam, overlap, identity, mask, or ArcGIS QA. This is feasibility arithmetic, not a request plan or a processing-unit estimate. A general CDSE user is documented at 10,000 Sentinel Hub processing units per month and 300 per minute, but this owner's remaining balance and actual task cost are unknown. Radiometric terrain correction carries a 2.5 processing-unit multiplier; output bands, data type, samples, and tile dimensions also affect charge. The Basic account cannot assume access to Sentinel Hub Batch Processing. [CDSE FAQ output limit](https://documentation.dataspace.copernicus.eu/FAQ.html) · [quotas](https://documentation.dataspace.copernicus.eu/Quotas.html) · [processing-unit rules](https://documentation.dataspace.copernicus.eu/APIs/SentinelHub/Overview/ProcessingUnit.html)

The Sentinel Hub API requires its own OAuth client ID and secret registered in the owner's account; the existing CDSE OData token handoff is **not** established as a compatible credential. Its documented Sentinel-1 filters include date and orbit direction, but this research has not established a guaranteed exact-granule selector for M1-SRC-002/005 or confirmed that the two 2026 Sentinel-1D scenes are present in its processing backend. No OAuth client, token, Catalog request, or Process request was created or used here. These account and scene-identity gaps make CDSE a secondary feasibility route, not an approved fallback. [authentication](https://documentation.dataspace.copernicus.eu/APIs/SentinelHub/Overview/Authentication.html) · [S1GRD filters](https://documentation.dataspace.copernicus.eu/APIs/SentinelHub/Data/S1GRD.html)

For a future owner-reviewed packet, the current **unapproved candidate** is one full-scene job per exact acquisition, in before/after order, each with `resolution=10.0`, `dem_name=copernicus`, `radiometry=gamma0`, `scale=decibel`, `speckle_filter=false`, and `dem_matching=false`; include the processing DEM, incidence map, and scattering-area map for provenance/QA, and omit the optional RGB. Keeping the provider's Enhanced Lee filter off avoids presenting it as the locally approved `REFINED_LEE` method; it leaves a separate speckle/temporal-comparison method question before quantitative change. Both jobs would use identical settings and public, non-sensitive job names. The exact candidate JSON is in the schema observation, **not submitted**. These options must be reviewed against the target map and capacity before any job. [ASF options](https://hyp3-docs.asf.alaska.edu/guides/rtc_product_guide/)

This is not a passing source gate. The unknown and unresolved criteria block an order under the current authority, while leaving public metadata analysis available.

- **Identity and provenance:** The proposed inputs would remain the two exact Sentinel-1D GRD acquisitions. The outputs would be new ASF HyP3 GAMMA RTC products, each with its own job and generated product identity, orbit provenance, processing settings, and checksum. They cannot inherit the source ID, scientific method, or admission state of the local ArcGIS-derived outputs.
- **Access and cost:** HyP3 Basic requires the owner's Earthdata Login and any applicable ASF end-user setup. ASF currently documents 8,000 free credits per month and 60 credits for each 10 m full-scene RTC job, so two such jobs would require 120 credits if the account and product are eligible. Do not submit or purchase anything from this estimate. Outputs are retained for 14 days. [HyP3 Basic](https://hyp3-docs.asf.alaska.edu/about/hyp3_basic/) · [credits](https://hyp3-docs.asf.alaska.edu/using/credits/) · [ASF SearchAPI access](https://docs.asf.alaska.edu/api/basics/)
- **Privacy:** ASF says all submitted jobs, including job name and user ID, are publicly visible and their products may be downloadable by others. Owner awareness and a non-sensitive job name are prerequisites. [HyP3 usage](https://hyp3-docs.asf.alaska.edu/using/)
- **Rights:** NASA's general open-data guidance does not replace an exact output-product rights and ASF account-terms check. The Earthdata setup may ask the owner to accept the ASF end-user agreement; this note does not accept it or assert unrestricted redistribution of a future product. ASF asks that a publication use the acknowledgement supplied in each product's README. [NASA data-use guidance](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/engage/open-data-services-software/data-use-policy) · [ASF access setup](https://docs.asf.alaska.edu/api/basics/) · [HyP3 citation guidance](https://hyp3-docs.asf.alaska.edu/usage_guidelines/)
- **Integrity and fitness:** An approved order would need exact job settings, immutable job/product IDs, provider manifest, per-file hashes, format/header validation, non-Git custody, and a fixed stop-on-failure plan. Actual EPSG:32645 grid, finite gamma values, VV/VH presence, layover/shadow exclusions, both-AOI coverage, registration, and repeatable ArcGIS export must be checked on the resulting bytes. Output spacing alone does not prove 10 m information, and catalog footprint does not prove usable pixels.

## Scientific-method differences to decide explicitly

ASF's GAMMA RTC uses its own processing and Copernicus GLO-30 DEM, whereas the approved local path uses ArcGIS terrain correction with the project's eleven-tile ellipsoidal DEM and `REFINED_LEE` sequence. HyP3's optional filter is Enhanced Lee; it must not be silently labeled `REFINED_LEE`. A candidate pair should use the **same provider, algorithm, radiometry, scale, pixel spacing, filter choice, and masking rules on both dates**. Existing local radar thresholds may remain useful as proposed checks, but their applicability to the ASF RTC mask and output needs a fresh method review. Even a pixel-QA pass would be `PASS_QA_ONLY`, not baseline admission or detected event change. [ASF processing options](https://hyp3-docs.asf.alaska.edu/guides/rtc_product_guide/) · [existing radar QA contract](../config/qa/pixel-readiness-contract.json)

## Next decision boundary

The on-demand ASF full-scene route is now the stronger **map-production hypothesis** than another unbounded local GTC repair: it directly targets geocoded GIS rasters for the same two dates and avoids the observed native-GTC resource stop. It is not ready to execute. A single owner-reviewed packet would need to settle the new method and source status, exact HyP3 options, account/terms/public-job visibility, current eligibility and credits, rights, a one-attempt-per-scene order and custody plan, and a separate RTC-specific QA and ArcGIS-map gate. The owner could instead prioritize local GTC engineering; neither route has a proven event-change layer. No old radar attempt or product is retried, reused, or overwritten here.
