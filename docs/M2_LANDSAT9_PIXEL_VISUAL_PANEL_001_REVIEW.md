## What is now established

The exact 10 August and 26 August Landsat-9 Collection 2 Level-2 full bundles passed container and group-aware metadata verification and were promoted without replacement to non-Git custody. The [terminal reconciliation](../records/readiness/m2-landsat9-grouped-mtl-recovery-002-terminal-reconciliation.json) binds the before TAR at **1,126,565,376 bytes**, SHA-256 `9f9a5a63e7abd78f889c9f5229a80f62008b6c31e88b212bf8af01950ca9b255`, and the after TAR at **1,123,888,128 bytes**, SHA-256 `f2b7a99f75d1324f0c7a984af5172740ca20862b774e2a17714df0cd7c454b38`. The [post-CI reconciliation](../records/readiness/m2-landsat9-grouped-mtl-recovery-002-post-ci-reconciliation.json) closes that intake envelope. Neither provider checksum equivalence nor TIFF fitness was established.

The public USGS [QA bit guide](https://www.usgs.gov/landsat-missions/landsat-collection-2-quality-assessment-bands) defines cloud, shadow, snow, saturation, terrain occlusion and aerosol flags. Its `QA_PIXEL` Clear bit only says the cloud and dilated-cloud bits are unset; it does not clear shadow or snow. USGS [surface-reflectance guidance](https://www.usgs.gov/landsat-missions/landsat-collection-2-surface-reflectance) gives `SR = DN × 0.0000275 − 0.2`. The [known-issues page](https://www.usgs.gov/landsat-missions/landsat-collection-2-known-issues) warns about aerosol overcorrection near bright snow/ice and cloud, values outside physical reflectance bounds, and band NoData missed by the QA band. These are reasons to inspect actual pixels and keep masks visible; they do not predict how many usable cells these scenes contain.

## One proposed conditional route

The [proposal](../contracts/milestone-002-landsat9-pixel-visual-panel-001-proposal.json) binds only the two promoted archives, the approved `AOI-SOURCE` and `AOI-UPPER-CORRIDOR`, and EPSG:32645. It would first verify the archived bytes and materialize only the ten named MTL, QA, and B3–B7 files per date in new non-Git custody. Header inspection must confirm the actual 30 m projected grids, compatible lattice, nodata and radiometric scale. The catalog's 40-column offset is a hypothesis until the acquired headers pass. A mismatch stops before pixel reads.

On header pass, the method would count before, after and paired valid areas against the **whole approved AOI**. It excludes cloud, cirrus, shadow, snow/ice, saturation, terrain occlusion, fill/NoData, nonphysical reflectance and the explicitly specified aerosol-risk conjunction. It reports overlapping raw flags plus one first-failure reason for area accounting. Interpolated aerosol and invalid retrieval alone remain visible uncertainty strata, since automatically rejecting all interpolation could erase documented LaSRC gap-filled pixels. Water remains a class to report, not an automatic exclusion. The unchanged 0.99 footprint, 0.80 full-usable and 0.20 partial-review thresholds are fixed before observation. A panel can be made only for an AOI with both dates' footprint at least 0.99 and paired strict-valid fraction at least 0.20; a value below 0.80 receives a prominent partial/incomplete label. If neither AOI qualifies, the route stops with QA evidence and no panel.

For qualifying AOIs only, one owner-local ArcGIS Pro project would show **B7/B6/B4 as RGB** for each date using a shared, deterministic pooled stretch. Both panels would use the same projected extent and visible exclusion classes. The APRX must reopen in a fresh process without broken paths, and its PNG/PDF exports must be inspected. This would be a spatially projected, exportable **visual comparison**. A common raster lattice is not measured residual co-registration; no difference raster, mapped change feature, baseline admission, event interpretation or attribution follows. The existing [pixel contract](../config/qa/pixel-readiness-contract.json) still requires at least 30 stable control pairs and at most 0.5-pixel RMSE and absolute bias for a later registration pass. The [Landsat route-gap record](../records/readiness/m2-landsat9-change-route-contract-gap-001-local.json) shows that the current M4 change route names Sentinel sources and a 20 m grid, not this 30 m Landsat pair.

The owner decision requested is **one bounded envelope**, not a sequence of intermediate reconfirmations: source-safe packet publication, versioned implementation and synthetic plus installed-ArcGIS tests, public CI, final no-content preflight, one fixed-order real QA attempt per exact date, at most one conditional local visual-panel attempt, and sanitized terminal publication. It allows no new download, retry, pixel threshold tuning, quantitative change analysis, derived-pixel publication, final-package claim, or scientific publication. Approval of this review would not make any real pixel outcome pass in advance.
