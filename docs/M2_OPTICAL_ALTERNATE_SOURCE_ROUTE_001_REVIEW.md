# Alternative optical sources: search through local visual QA

**Local review only. No new source request, download, pixel work or publication is released by this draft.** The long-term objective remains mapped satellite-observed change and a reproducible ArcGIS evidence package. A shortlist or visual panel alone would not complete it.

## Why another source route is needed

The exact 10 and 26 August Landsat-9 products passed metadata, headers and native EPSG:32645 grid checks, but paired strict usable coverage was **0%** in the source AOI and **0.1777%** in the upper corridor, below the unchanged 20% visual floor. The [published terminal record](../records/readiness/m2-landsat9-visual-route-completion-001-terminal-reconciliation.json) preserves that result and consumed attempt. Neither exact source can be requeued, given relaxed masks or retried to force a map.

The existing partial ASF radar panel is visual only. Its proposed difference route was [withdrawn](../records/readiness/m2-asf-reference-control-vv-delta-map-002-pre-approval-withdrawal.json) after known synthetic shifts falsely passed registration; a later [held-out screen](../records/readiness/m2-asf-registration-boundary-screen-004-local-reconciliation.json) also falsified the combined estimator. This packet cannot reactivate either method.

The [updated completion audit](../records/readiness/goal-completion-audit-20261003-local-002.json) retains the full requirements: credible sources, mapped change with uncertainty, observation/interpretation/attribution separation, failed history, five named maps, required datasets and relationships, exports and an independent package round trip. Those scientific and final-delivery requirements remain unverified.

## Prospectively delegated source choice

The [proposal](../contracts/milestone-002-optical-alternate-source-route-001-proposal.json) searches Landsat-8 and Landsat-9 Collection 2 Level-2 Tier-1 metadata at path/row **141/040**, retaining both approved focus AOIs. Fixed windows are **27 July–25 August 2026 before** and **27 August–25 September 2026 after**. Excluding the event day avoids within-day timing ambiguity. Wider temporal separation retains weather, snow/ice, vegetation and unrelated-change uncertainty; it does not strengthen event attribution.

Four platform/window queries run in fixed order. At most eight search pages, four selected-item details, two catalog/conformance reads and two official rights/distribution reads are permitted, within an **18-request metadata cap** including at most two bounded transient recoveries. Incomplete pagination, access denial, changed terms, identity inconsistency or unexpected hosts stop selection. Current access and catalog availability have not been checked in this preparation.

Eligible pairs must use the same sensor and path/row, have complete identities/dates, and have catalog footprints containing both focus polygons. Rank by maximum reported scene cloud cover, combined cloud cover, acquisition interval and product IDs. Missing cloud fields remain unknown; the two previously tested products remain terminal exclusions. **Scene cloud and catalog footprints do not prove usable AOI pixels.** No dates, geometry or ranking may change after results. Lock the complete inventory, rejections and top two pairs before source payloads.

Approval would delegate this exact selection policy. It would not require another source-choice confirmation for each candidate whose recorded identity and rights satisfy it. An incomplete or ambiguous inventory cannot activate acquisition.

## Bounded intake, unchanged QA, one conditional map

Use **EarthExplorer full Level-2 Product Bundles only**, the previously selected host. Acquire at most **four distinct new products**, in pair-rank order and before/after order. The cap is **six full-bundle activations**: four initial products and two classified transfer/handoff recoveries across the whole route. Each archive is capped at **2 GiB**; promoted originals total at most **8 GiB**, and all received source bytes including partials at most **12 GiB**. At least **30 GiB** free disk is required. No paid data, new account, agent credential collection, installation, terms acceptance or alternate provider is authorized. If a tool cannot operate the exact approved download control, the owner may need to perform that click as a runtime handoff, not another source decision.

The weaker browser-to-file provenance method is explicitly proposed for local QA custody: pre-download inventory, exact product intent, observed transfer/name/size/stability, then full TAR/member hashes and strict grouped product/scene/date verification. Ambiguous, preexisting, multiple or mismatching files stop. A local checksum is not upstream authentication. Preserve partials and failed attempts; promote only into fresh non-Git no-replace custody.

After synthetic and installed ArcGIS tests, public CI and no-content gates, audit metadata before TIFFs, all pair headers before pixels, and evaluate the same ten members, native **30 m EPSG:32645** grid, B3–B7 calibration, QA masks, exact AOI-area denominators and display stretch as the approved Landsat visual core. No thresholds or science predicates are relaxed. Full visual QA requires both dates' footprint at least 99%, and single-date and paired strict usability at least 80% at both AOIs. Partial visual eligibility still requires 99% footprint and at least 20% paired usability at an AOI.

Evaluate pair 1 first. If it reaches full visual QA at both AOIs, do not acquire pair 2. Otherwise evaluate the next already-locked pair only when integrity and safety gates permit. Select the first full-QA pair in rank order; if neither is full, select the first ranked partial-qualifying pair; if neither qualifies, create no map. Insufficient coverage is retained as exclusion/defer, not repaired by changing the source list or QA. Identity, calibration, grid, rights or unclassified failure is a hard stop.

At most one qualifying local APRX would contain georeferenced sources, inspectable exclusions, writable defaults, UTC dates, coverage, credits and visible partial-data/unmeasured-registration warnings, with PNG/PDF exports, fresh-process reopen, broken-path checks and rendered inspection. **No difference raster or scientific change claim is permitted.**

## One approval through this route outcome

Approval covers packet/decision publication, tested implementation, public CI, metadata selection, bounded intake, fresh append-only supervised QA, the conditional local visual map and sanitized terminal publication. Deterministically classified mechanical corrections inherit the same scope after preserved failures, relevant tests, fresh CI when code changes and fresh preflight. Two consecutive failures without progress require an internal evidence review. Acquisition recovery stays inside the request/byte budget. Routine steps do not require another owner confirmation.

The outcome must also leave a scientific next-milestone brief. Existing M4 optical rules bind Sentinel-2 at 20 m and cannot silently transfer route identities or spectral thresholds to Landsat at 30 m. Passing visual QA would still leave registration, stable controls, Landsat-specific change methods, reviewed evidence links, M5 owner review and M6 delivery unresolved. This packet grants none of that scientific adoption.

## Decision

Approve, revise or defer this **single source-to-visual-readiness envelope**, including prospective selection under the fixed policy and the documented weaker local browser provenance. It preserves the existing goal and every prior failure. It grants no old-source retry, altered masks, baseline admission, change analysis, attribution, public derived pixels or final scientific delivery. An empty, blocked or inconclusive route is an acceptable recorded outcome.
