## Map objective and present boundary

The project needs an editable ArcGIS Pro before/after map in EPSG:32645 that distinguishes the source area and upper corridor, preserves exclusions and uncertainty, and can be exported. The approved AOIs and a terrain context map exist. They are not a measured event-change map. No candidate pair has passed real pixel and registration QA, and no baseline or change layer has been admitted.

The strongest **catalog-footprint** match remains ascending Sentinel-1 M1-SRC-002/005: both source and upper-corridor AOIs have 100% footprint overlap. That number does not measure usable radar pixels. The one M1-SRC-002 processing attempt is consumed. Its saved GTC rasters report EPSG:4326, 2 × 2 cells at 10 degrees per cell, rather than the frozen EPSG:32645, 10 m grid. The target grid has 78,161,896 cells. A projection of a coarse 2 × 2 raster cannot recover radar detail that was not retained. ## The method question to resolve first

Esri describes Apply Geometric Terrain Correction as range-Doppler backgeocoding over DEM grid points, with a DEM recommended over land. Its documented environments include Snap Raster and Cell Alignment, but do not list Cell Size, Output Coordinate System, or Extent. The existing runner set `cellSize=10.0` and a snap raster around GTC, while the saved GTC result reports 10-degree cells. The 11-tile DEM mosaic itself reports 14,400 × 10,800 cells in EPSG:4326, so the GTC result cannot simply be assumed to inherit that mosaic's grid. These facts identify a method risk; they do not establish why the output became 2 × 2 or prove a fix. [Method audit](../records/observations/m2-radar-gtc-environment-method-audit-001.json) · [DEM mosaic receipt](../records/processing/m2-radar-event-area-pair-001-dem-mosaic-reconciliation.json) · [Esri GTC documentation](https://doc.esri.com/en/arcgis-pro/latest/tool-reference/image-analyst/apply-geometric-terrain-correction.html)

The earlier disposable projection route exceeded its 100 m extent guard by several kilometres. The projected-clip route retained valid synthetic pixels but started 900 m east of the frozen western boundary. Those failed tests rule out reusing either candidate as-is; they do not prove that all projected-grid methods are impossible. ### Narrowed native-grid hypothesis

A code-and-receipt audit adds one testable lead. Around each GTC call, the runner supplies a projected 10 m snap raster and numeric `cellSize=10.0`; it does not set a GTC output CRS there. The saved output is EPSG:4326 with exactly 10-degree cells. Its 20-degree horizontal and vertical spans equal two cells times 10 degrees. That numeric match suggests a geographic-versus-metric grid-unit interaction, but it does **not** prove that GTC honored Cell Size, that the snap raster caused the result, or that removing either setting fixes it. Esri's Cell Size guidance says numeric values are not projected for tools that honor that environment, while GTC does not list Cell Size among its documented environments. [Unit hypothesis and exact arithmetic](../records/observations/m2-gtc-grid-unit-hypothesis-001.json) · [Esri Cell Size guidance](https://doc.esri.com/en/arcgis-pro/latest/tool-reference/environment-settings/cell-size.html)

The candidate for a later bounded method review is to isolate *native* GTC from the meter-valued snap and numeric cell-size settings, retain the exact DEM and SAR sequence, inspect native grid and effective resolution before projection, then project only a validated output to the frozen grid. This is still a **design hypothesis**, not a correction ready to run. The [native-GTC method design](M2_NATIVE_GTC_GRID_METHOD_DESIGN_001.md) specifies environment isolation, a pre-save metadata check, and stop conditions; the [local recovery-003 proposal](../contracts/milestone-002-radar-event-area-pair-native-gtc-grid-recovery-003-proposal.json) fixes proposed native-grid and resource limits for an owner decision. Full processed-extent valid DEM coverage and SAR-specific behavior remain unproven. Generic synthetic raster projection can test grid mechanics but cannot validate GTC's SAR geolocation.

## Recommended next work and stop rules

1. **Design, without preserved-data access, a concrete source-specific method candidate.** Specify how ArcGIS GTC receives full-extent valid DEM elevation; how native GTC resolution and georeferencing are established; and how the result would become the exact EPSG:32645, 10 m grid without inventing detail. Bound cell count, disk, path lengths, and output extent before a run. Record why it differs from both closed disposable candidates.
2. **Test only what disposable inputs can actually establish.** Generated raster tests can check coordinate transforms, target coverage, mask resampling, pixel preservation, resource limits, and receipt durability. They cannot establish that GTC will generate correct SAR geometry, valid elevation over the real swaths, or real event pixels. A SAR-specific test needs its own exact inputs and authority.
3. **Return with one coherent bounded method decision** only after the candidate, inputs, validations, and failure stops are explicit. If no defensible candidate emerges, record that and revisit the descending source-only or alternate optical route as a new decision. Do not consume another real radar attempt to discover the method.
